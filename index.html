Privacy Policy — Tablet Reminder

Last updated: 12 November 2026

Tablet Reminder ("the app") is a medicine reminder app.

Data stored on your device. The tablet names, times and dose history you enter are stored only on your phone. We do not collect, upload or have access to this data. A backup file is created only if you press "Save backup", and you choose where it goes.

Advertising. The app shows ads using Google AdMob. AdMob may collect and use the device's advertising ID, IP address and general device information to show ads and measure them. See Google's policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy . You can reset or delete your advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.

Permissions. Notifications, exact alarms, full-screen alerts, vibration, wake lock and run-at-startup are used only to ring your medicine alarms on time. Internet access is used only for ads.

Children. The app is not directed to children under 13.

Medical disclaimer. The app is a reminder tool and does not provide medical advice.

Changes. We may update this policy; the date above will change.
